This project examines how demographic characteristics and investment behaviors are associated with equity market investment preferences using the Finance Trends dataset. Statistical summaries, visualizations, chi-square tests, and logistic regression were used to identify factors related to ranking the equity market among the top two investment choices. The results show that age, gender, and savings objective were associated with equity preference, while several other factors were not.
